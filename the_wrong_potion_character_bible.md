# MAIN CHARACTER

## Aldric Vane (Player Character)

**Default Name:** Aldric Vane (player can rename)
**Age:** 26
**Race:** Human
**Occupation:** Sellsword / Freelance Mercenary
**Origin:** Farmboy from the village of Dunmore, two weeks north of Valdmere. Family land was seized by a local lord when Aldric was 19 — father died shortly after from "grief and drink," mother moved in with relatives. Aldric took up the sword because it was the only trade that didn't require land, a guild membership, or a conscience.

### Physical Appearance (Day 0 — Pre-Transformation)

**Height:** 6'1"
**Weight:** 195 lbs
**Build:** Athletic, broad-shouldered, muscular but not bulky. A working fighter's body — built for endurance and power, not vanity. Thick deltoids, defined pectorals, visible abdominals. Strong legs, thick forearms from years of swordwork.
**Hair:** Dark brown, short and messy, perpetually looking like he just woke up. Stray strands fall over his forehead. Grows quickly.
**Eyes:** Deep-set, sharp green with hooded lids. His most distinctive feature — intense, watchful, the kind of eyes that make people feel seen whether they want to be or not.
**Face:** Rugged, handsome in a rough-hewn way. Pronounced square jawline. High cheekbones. Straight nose with a slight bump on the bridge (broken once in a bar fight, healed crooked). Thick dark eyebrows. Full lower lip with a thinner upper lip. A characteristic crooked half-smile that pulls more to the right side.
**Skin:** Tanned, weathered from years of outdoor living. Rough texture, especially on hands and forearms.
**Stubble:** Light brown, kept to a perpetual three-day scruff because he's too lazy to shave daily and too vain to grow a full beard.
**Scars:**
- Small diagonal scar cutting through the left eyebrow (knife fight, age 21 — the scar that persists through the entire transformation, the last physical link to his old self)
- Battle scar along the right ribcage (Thornwall job, stitched by Mira)
- Thin scar on left outer thigh (dire rat bite, the job they don't talk about)
- Diagonal scar across right shoulder blade (ambush on the road to Kestwick)
**Body hair:** Moderate. Dark chest hair concentrated on the pectorals with a trail down the midline of the abdomen. Hairy forearms. Light hair on legs. Dark pubic hair.
**Hands:** Large, calloused, thick fingers. A swordsman's hands — strong grip, rough palms, nicks and small scars across the knuckles.
**Voice:** Deep baritone. Speaks with a slight rural drawl that he's never quite lost despite years away from Dunmore.
**Adam's apple:** Prominent, visible when he swallows or tilts his head.
**Genitalia:** Average length, uncircumcised penis. Full testicles.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Stubborn, dry-humored, self-deprecating, loyal, pragmatic. Not particularly clever but not dumb either — street-smart rather than book-smart. Has a habit of talking to himself when stressed, which becomes an effective internal monologue device during the transformation.

**Strengths:** Reliable in a fight. Honest to a fault (which is a liability as often as it's an asset). Brave bordering on reckless. Genuinely kind underneath the mercenary exterior — tips barmaids, gives coins to beggars, once walked three miles out of his way to return a lost dog.

**Weaknesses:** Terrible with money. Makes decisions emotionally and justifies them rationally after the fact. Avoids introspection like it's a disease — which makes the forced self-examination of the transformation particularly excruciating for him. Drinks too much when stressed. Trusts too easily.

**Fears:** Losing himself — not just physically, but the person he knows how to be. Heights (mildly, won't admit it). Ending up like his father — broken, bitter, drinking himself into a grave.

**Attitude toward the transformation:** Progresses through phases — denial, panic, anger, bargaining, gradual exploration, and eventual reckoning with whether the person in the mirror is someone worth being. The player's choices determine where on the acceptance spectrum he ultimately lands.

**Fighting style:** Longsword, one-handed or two-handed depending on the situation. Trained informally — no academy, learned from other mercenaries and hard experience. Favors economy of motion over flashiness. Strong, direct, adaptable. As the transformation progresses, his fighting style must evolve as his body changes — from power-based to speed-based, from strength to finesse.

### Relationships

**Mira Dunhollow:** Traveling companion, business partner, and bedmate. They've been sleeping together for approximately two months (since Kestwick). Neither of them has defined the relationship or used words like "love." The arrangement is: travel together, fight together, split earnings, fuck when they want to, don't talk about feelings. It works. Until his body starts changing and every unspoken thing between them demands to be spoken.

**Family:** Father dead (drink). Mother alive but distant, living with her sister in a village he hasn't visited in two years. A younger brother, **Cael**, who apprenticed to a blacksmith and wants nothing to do with the mercenary life. Aldric sends money home when he has it. He usually doesn't have it.

# COMPANION CHARACTERS

**Face:** Sharp-featured. Angular jaw, high forehead, pronounced cheekbones. A face that's more striking than conventionally pretty — arresting rather than soft. Thin lips that she presses together when thinking.
**Skin:** Tawny — a warm, golden-brown tone inherited from the blend of her father's ruddy complexion and her mother's elven pallor. Smooth, with a few small scars on her hands and forearms.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Sharp-tongued, pragmatic, fiercely competent, emotionally guarded. Communicates affection through action rather than words — she'll stitch your wound and call you six kinds of idiot while doing it, and that's her version of saying she cares. Picks her teeth with an arrowhead when thinking (nervous habit she's had since childhood).

**Strengths:** Exceptional archer — possibly the best Aldric has ever seen. Quick reflexes. Reads situations instantly. Reliable, once her loyalty is earned. Has a dry, cutting humor that ranges from hilarious to devastating depending on how much she likes you.

**Weaknesses:** Emotionally constipated. Uses sarcasm as a defense mechanism. Struggles to express vulnerability. Has a deep-seated fear of being abandoned (rooted in her dual rejection — not elven enough for elves, not human enough for humans). Overcompensates for this by maintaining emotional distance and refusing to define relationships.

**Attitude toward the short sword:** She hates it. The balance is wrong. The grip smells like old cheese. It has saved her life twice and she resents it for both.

### Relationship with Aldric

Their arrangement started in Kestwick — a shoulder rub that escalated into sex after the dwarf arm-wrestling incident. Neither of them planned it. Neither of them stopped it. It became a pattern: hard day, shared bed, no discussion. Mira takes the top in bed (always), bites the same spot on his neck (always), and never stays to cuddle (usually — except when she does, which is more often lately, which neither of them has addressed).

She knows Aldric's body intimately — every scar, every muscle group, every spot that makes him groan. This becomes the central tension of Branch B during the transformation: the body she memorized through months of intimate contact is changing under her hands, and she has to reconcile the person she knows with the person emerging.

Her reaction to the transformation progresses through: denial and practical problem-solving (early days), confusion and discomfort (middle days), genuine crisis of attraction and identity (late days), and eventual reckoning with whether what she feels is for the body or the person inside it.

### Combat

Longbow primary. Short sword secondary (the cheese-smelling one). Fighting style: ranged precision at distance, vicious close-quarters efficiency when forced into melee. Prefers to fight from elevated or concealed positions. In close combat, relies on speed and half-elf agility rather than strength.

## Ysolde — "The Cistern Witch"

**Age:** Unknown. Appears ~50 but could be older. Something about her doesn't age the way it should.
**Race:** Human (allegedly)
**Occupation:** Underground healer / Alchemist / Something else entirely
**Location:** Operates out of a converted cellar in the cistern district of Valdmere's Undercity

### Physical Appearance

**Height:** 5'10" — tall for a woman, nearly Aldric's height
**Weight:** ~140 lbs
**Build:** Thin, angular, austere. Not frail — there's wire-strength under the leanness, the body of someone who moves precisely and doesn't waste energy.
**Hair:** Iron-gray, pulled back from her face in a severe braid that hangs over one shoulder. Was probably dark once — black or very dark brown.
**Eyes:** Dark — so dark that in lamplight, the iris is indistinguishable from the pupil. Watchful, evaluating, patient. The eyes of someone who sees more than she says.
**Face:** Angular, austere, striking. High cheekbones, sharp nose, thin lips that rarely smile and when they do, the smile doesn't quite reach her eyes. She was beautiful once, in a severe way. Age has hardened the beauty into something more interesting — less approachable, more magnetic.
**Skin:** Pale, despite living underground. Fine lines around the eyes and mouth. Hands are surprisingly elegant — long-fingered, steady, the hands of a surgeon or a musician.
**Notable feature:** A bracelet on her left wrist made of small glass vials, each containing a different colored liquid. They clink softly when she moves, like tiny wind chimes.
**Clothing:** Simple linen dress under a leather apron stained with substances of various colors. Practical, clean despite the stains. No jewelry except the vial bracelet.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Calm, precise, enigmatic, patient. Speaks in measured sentences. Never hurries. Treats injured bodies the way a master craftsman treats raw material — with professional respect and absolute confidence. Answers questions obliquely or not at all, which is deeply unsettling when you're the one drinking her potions.

**What she's hiding:** Ysolde's connection to the transformation is deliberately ambiguous in the early game. Possibilities include:
- She is Vex's mentor, teacher, or former partner
- She is Vex herself, under a different identity
- She is a genuine healer who unknowingly used one of Vex's compounds
- She is something older and stranger — connected to the goddess whose potion recipe Vex stole
- She knew exactly what the "restorative compound" would do and chose Aldric specifically

**Her role:** She appears only briefly in the prologue but can recur throughout the game as a resource, a mystery, and potentially an antagonist or ally depending on the player's investigation path. Finding Ysolde again and getting answers is one of the game's secondary quest lines.

**Her smile:** The small, closed-lipped expression she gives Aldric as he drinks the vial. Not predatory. Not kind. Patient. Like a gardener watching a seed.

# ROMANCE CHARACTERS

## Sera — The Herbalist

**Age:** 29
**Race:** Human
**Occupation:** Apothecary owner in the Undercity
**Location:** Runs a shop called "The Green Lung" in the Undercity market district

### Physical Appearance

**Height:** 5'5"
**Weight:** ~145 lbs
**Build:** Soft, curvy, comfortable. Not athletic — she spends her days grinding herbs and reading books, and her body reflects a life of the mind rather than the sword. Full breasts, wide hips, thick waist. She moves with surprising grace for someone who seems perpetually distracted.
**Hair:** Black, thick, perpetually escaping whatever bun or braid she's attempted. Loose curls that frame her face. She has a habit of tucking stray strands behind her ear while she's thinking, which is constantly.
**Eyes:** Dark brown, large, magnified slightly by the round brass spectacles she wears. Intensely focused — when Sera looks at something, she *looks* at it with the forensic attention of someone trying to understand its molecular structure.
**Face:** Round, warm, expressive. Olive skin with a beauty mark below her left eye. Full lips that she bites when concentrating. Heavy dark eyebrows that she's never bothered to groom.
**Skin:** Olive-toned, smooth. Her fingers are perpetually stained — green from herbs, blue from ink, occasionally yellow from sulfur compounds. She smells like lavender and something sharper underneath, chemical and clean.
**Clothing:** Practical apothecary wear — linen blouse, leather apron with dozens of pockets, heavy skirt hemmed high enough to move in. Everything has herb stains.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Brilliant, obsessive, socially awkward, accidentally insensitive, deeply caring underneath. A scientist's brain in a healer's body. She cares about people but she cares about *understanding* people more, which makes her bedside manner simultaneously impressive and terrible.

**Strengths:** Encyclopedic knowledge of herbs, potions, anatomy, and alchemical theory. Can identify compounds by smell. Genuinely wants to help people — runs a sliding-scale pricing system for the Undercity's poor. Brave in unexpected ways — not in combat, but in her willingness to investigate things others would leave alone.

**Weaknesses:** Talks too much when excited. Asks invasive questions without realizing they're invasive. Gets so absorbed in research that she forgets basic things like eating and sleeping. Struggles with social cues.

### Romance Dynamic

Sera is the first person Aldric goes to for answers about the potion. Her fascination with the transformation is initially clinical — she wants to study it, document it, understand the alchemical mechanisms at work. This feels invasive and dehumanizing to Aldric at first.

But the clinical interest evolves into genuine fascination with the *person* undergoing the change, not just the change itself. Sera begins seeing Aldric — not the transformation, but the person navigating it. The shift from subject to individual to someone she's attracted to is the arc of her romance.

**Sexuality:** Bisexual. Has dated men and women. The transformation doesn't create a crisis for her — it creates curiosity, then attraction, then something deeper.

**Physical intimacy with Aldric:** The eroticism of their relationship is rooted in being *seen*. Sera wants to observe, touch, catalog every change. She asks permission. She describes what she sees. She makes the transformation visible in a way that's simultaneously vulnerable and arousing. Their sex scenes are detailed, anatomically specific, and charged with the intensity of someone being known completely.

## Lorn — The Old Comrade

**Age:** 30
**Race:** Human
**Occupation:** Mercenary / Former member of the same company as Aldric
**Origin:** From the northern mining town of Greyhollow. Grew up underground, came to the surface to fight, never went back.

### Physical Appearance

**Height:** 6'3"
**Weight:** 220 lbs
**Build:** Massive. Built like a siege engine — broad chest, thick arms, heavy legs, a neck like a tree trunk. Not fast, not graceful, but immovable and devastating in close combat. Carries a two-handed warhammer that most people couldn't lift with both arms.

**Skin:** Fair, ruddy, freckled across the nose and cheeks. Sun-damaged from years of outdoor work. A lattice of small scars across his knuckles and forearms.
**Voice:** Deep, quiet, measured. Speaks only when he has something to say, which isn't often. When he does speak, people listen, because they know the words were chosen carefully.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Loyal, quiet, protective, emotionally repressed. The kind of man who expresses affection through acts of service rather than words — he'll sharpen your blade, watch your back, carry your pack, and never mention it. Comfortable with silence in a way that makes most people uncomfortable.

### Romance Dynamic

Lorn shows up in Valdmere on Day 5, looking for work. He recognizes Aldric immediately — and keeps recognizing her no matter how much she changes. His feelings were always there, hidden under the safe framework of male friendship. The transformation doesn't create his desire — it removes the barriers he was using to hide it.

**Sexuality:** Bisexual, deeply closeted, has never acted on his attraction to men (or to Aldric specifically). The transformation forces him to confront feelings he's spent years burying.

**Physical intimacy with Aldric:** Tender, careful, reverent. Lorn touches the transforming body like it's something sacred and fragile, which both arouses and frustrates Aldric, who sometimes wants to be desired rather than handled with care. The tension between his gentleness and her need for passion is the erotic engine of their relationship. When he finally lets go — when she pulls him past his restraint — it's intense and emotional and wrecked with years of suppression.

## Vex — The Alchemist

**Age:** 34
**Race:** Human (transitioned — MTF, via her own alchemical creation)
**Occupation:** Alchemist / Fugitive / Creator of the transformation potion
**True Name:** Unknown. "Vex" is the name she chose for herself.

### Physical Appearance

**Height:** 5'6"
**Weight:** ~130 lbs
**Build:** Slender, angular, sharp. Her own transformation was self-administered and deliberately controlled — she chose the body she wanted with the precision of a sculptor. Lean, small-breasted, narrow-hipped by choice. She looks like a blade — beautiful and dangerous.
**Hair:** Black, straight, cut in a sharp asymmetrical bob — longer on the left side, shorter on the right. Dyed with alchemical compounds that give it an unnatural blue-black sheen.
**Eyes:** Heterochromatic — one dark brown (natural), one pale violet (alchemical side effect of self-experimentation). She doesn't hide the violet eye. She considers it a badge of honor.
**Face:** Sharp, foxlike, clever. High cheekbones, pointed chin, thin lips that curl naturally into a smirk. She looks like someone who knows a secret and enjoys knowing it.
**Skin:** Pale, almost porcelain. A network of very fine scars on her forearms — not self-harm, but the marks of years of alchemical work where compounds splashed, burned, or reacted unexpectedly.
**Hands:** Quick, precise, always moving. Stained like Sera's but differently — darker, more metallic. She wears rings on every finger, each one set with a different stone or compound.
**Clothing:** Dark, practical, layered. A long coat over fitted clothing, with dozens of interior pockets containing vials, tools, and ingredients. Leather boots with hidden compartments. She dresses like someone who might need to run at any moment.

### Personality

**Core traits:** Brilliant, amoral (not immoral — there's a distinction she'd insist on), charismatic, unapologetic. She believes the potion is a gift, not a curse, and she chose Aldric deliberately — not randomly. She saw something in him that she recognized, though what exactly that is depends on the player's choices and the story's revelations.

**Strengths:** Genius-level alchemist. Created the transformation potion through years of self-experimentation, failure, and refinement. Charismatic in a dangerous way — she makes you want to believe her even when you know you shouldn't. Fearless about her own identity.

**Weaknesses:** Arrogant about her work. Genuinely doesn't understand why Aldric might be upset about being transformed without consent — in her mind, she gave him something precious. This blind spot is her central flaw. She's empathetic about the *experience* of transformation but deaf to the violation of *choice*.

### Backstory

Born male in a port city to the south. Knew from childhood that her body was wrong. Discovered alchemy as a teenager and became obsessed with the idea of physical transformation. Spent fifteen years developing the potion — testing it on herself first, refining the formula through dozens of iterations, nearly killing herself twice. The version she sells (and that Aldric received from Ysolde) is the final, perfected formula.

She disappeared from Valdmere after distributing several doses through proxies (including, potentially, Ysolde). Her reasons for doing so — and her connection to Ysolde — are central mysteries of the game.

### Romance Dynamic

Romancing Vex requires finding her first (mid-game quest). When found, the relationship is volcanic — fueled by Aldric's anger at what she did and an undeniable attraction to the only person who truly understands what the transformation feels like.

**Sexuality:** Lesbian (post-transition). Attracted to women, including women who used to be men, because she sees herself in them.

**Physical intimacy with Aldric:** Rough, charged, dominant-switching, emotionally raw. Vex knows the transformed body from the inside — she's felt every change Aldric is feeling, because she felt them first. Sex with Vex is partly sex and partly confrontation — anger and desire tangled together, bodies that share a history neither of them chose communicating in ways words can't.

# WORLD DETAILS

## Valdmere

**Type:** Mid-sized trade city on a river delta
**Character:** Prosperous, corrupt, pragmatic. A city that runs on commerce and doesn't ask too many questions about where the money comes from. The upper city is clean, orderly, and controlled by merchant guilds. The lower city is chaotic, dirty, and controlled by whoever has the most muscle this week.
**Districts:**
- **Upper City** — Merchant estates, guild halls, the Ashmore estate, temples, the city garrison
- **Market District** — The commercial heart, shops and stalls, taverns of varying quality
- **Dockside** — Warehouses, shipping offices, the Consortium headquarters, rougher taverns
- **The Undercity** — The oldest district, built on ruins, home to black-market traders, unlicensed practitioners, and the city's less legitimate residents
- **The Cistern District** — The lowest point of the Undercity, built over the old water system. Perpetually damp. Where Ysolde operates.

## The Broken Antler Tavern

**Location:** Market District, near the east gate
**Character:** A working-class tavern. Low ceilings stained with pipe smoke, furniture more nail than wood, terrible ale. Not dangerous, just depressing. The kind of place where mercenaries drink between jobs and try not to think about their life choices.
**Innkeeper:** A heavyset man who barely looks up from his ledger. Tolerates everything as long as you pay your tab and don't break his furniture (again).

## The Greymire Mine

**Location:** Four leagues east of Valdmere, off the Ashenmoor Road, at the base of a rocky escarpment
**Character:** Abandoned iron mine, played out decades ago. Upper tunnels used as a goblin camp. Deeper shafts used for storage. Rotting timbers, unstable floors, narrow passages. The site of Aldric's injury that leads him to Ysolde and the potion.

# THEMATIC NOTES

## Central Themes

**Bodily autonomy:** The transformation was forced, but the response is the character's own. Every choice about presentation, identity, and desire belongs to the player.

**Intimacy as discovery:** Sex scenes are the character learning a new body — through solo exploration and through the hands of a partner. Each encounter reveals something new.

**The cure as question:** Finding the antidote isn't the climax. Deciding whether to drink it is.

**Being seen:** Each romance route explores a different facet of being known — clinically (Sera), historically (Lorn), by the person who did this (Vex), or with no context at all (Kael).

**The persistence of self:** The eyebrow scar remains throughout all 45 days. The green eyes remain. The crooked half-smile remains. The question the game asks: what makes you *you* — the body or the person inside it?
