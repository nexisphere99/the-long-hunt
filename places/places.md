# The Wrong Potion — Location Image Prompts

## Guide
Each prompt is designed for AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion). Style is consistent: **dark fantasy, painterly, medieval, moody lighting, muted greens and golds**.

Append your preferred style suffix to each prompt, e.g.:
- Midjourney: `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1`
- SDXL: `dark fantasy art, digital painting, highly detailed`
