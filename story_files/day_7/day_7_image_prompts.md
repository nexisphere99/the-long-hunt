The Ashenmoor Road continues east, broad and well-maintained; a narrower road branches northeast into thicker forest climbing toward distant foothills. A stocky, bearded man in practical traveling clothes (Hadden) extends his hand to shake with a taller man in leather armor (Aldric). Hadden holds a sealed envelope in his other hand. Behind them, a half-elf woman (Mira) stands with her bow, watching the farewell. A patient mule (Pebble) waits with loaded saddlebags. The morning light is warm and golden.
